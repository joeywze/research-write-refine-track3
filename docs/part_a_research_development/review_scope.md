# Step 1: targeted methods, experiments and limitations review

Prepared 20 October 2026. Scope: the 18 Cluster 7 papers, with deeper language-related checks for 86, 103, 138, 712, 891 and 1307. This is a source-grounded review for developing the proposal, not an independent replication, a numerical-results audit or an exhaustive systematic review. Page references below are one-based PDF pages.

The [claim-to-evidence table](claim_evidence_table.md) supplies exact passages and verification status for the findings used to constrain the gap. This overview paraphrases methodological context; it does not replace those exact passages. “Unclear” means the reviewed material does not establish language coverage, not that the paper is English-only.

## Verification boundaries and unresolved work

The cited source statements were checked in the reviewed pages; their statistical validity and experimental reproducibility were not independently established. This review does not certify every extracted sentence in the original automated files. Those files remain unchanged, and any finding not mapped to supporting text remains unverified.

For multilingual-gap development, response language, instruction language, source language, task/domain and human-reference coverage must remain separate fields. No unsupported “No” labels or universal high-/low-resource classifications are introduced.

Before a novelty claim, extend the comparison beyond this sample. The [external evidence review](external_evidence.md) includes close work that materially challenges the original broad gap.
