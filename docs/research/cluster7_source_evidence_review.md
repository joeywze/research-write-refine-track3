## How to read this review

- Paper-content entries below are direct quotations attributed to their authors. Short fragments are labelled as anchors, not complete arguments.
- PDF pages are one-based. Each quotation links to its exact PDF page; the full paper is linked in the heading.
- Line breaks and line-break hyphenation have been joined. The wording was checked against both the existing extracted text and a fresh extraction from the local PDF.
- Quote verification establishes that the authors wrote the passage. It does not independently validate the authors' experiments, conclusions or statistical claims.
- This is a targeted evidence review, not an experimental replication or a comprehensive literature search. No new research-gap or novelty claim is approved here.
- Language coverage is **Unclear from these excerpts** wherever the excerpts do not explicitly establish it. No language or resource-level absence is inferred from a missing keyword.
- The earlier automated coding files remain unchanged. This source review is a separate record, not a silent replacement of the original labels.
